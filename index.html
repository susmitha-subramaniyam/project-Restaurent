Our Philosophy

A Tradition of Excellence

Founded on the principles of omotenashi — the Japanese art of wholehearted hospitality — Yamato represents the pinnacle of traditional Japanese steakhouse dining in the United Kingdom.

Our master chefs, trained in the ancient traditions of teppanyaki, transform the finest ingredients into an unforgettable theatrical dining experience. Each cut of our premium A5 Wagyu beef is selected with meticulous care, ensuring the perfect balance of marbling and flavor.

From the moment you enter our doors, you are transported to Japan — where every detail, from the warm wooden interiors to the precise knife work of our chefs, reflects our commitment to authenticity and excellence.
